### When do I consider upgrading to Sectors Standard or Insider?

Sectors operate the Sectors Financial API, which is a developer-friendly RESTful API that provides access to all our comprehensive financial market data. Through the Sectors API, you can access Indonesian stock market data, sector reports, company reports, and financials right from your application, website, or product. Developers, financial analysts and businesses can use the Sectors API to build financial market tools, investment platforms, financial market applications, or build out workflows that require high-quality, quantitative financial market data. We offer this API as a part of our Standard and Insider plans, along with other quality-of-life features carefully designed to make your investment research and analysis more efficient, enjoyable and productive.

To help you make the most out of your subscription, all subscribers on Sectors Standard and Insider plans also have full complementary access to our monthly financial analytics workshops, conducted live and in-person by the very same analytics engineers building Sectors, this app you are using. These workshops are 3-hour programming deep dives into the financial data, analytics, and investment research tooling that we use in our own personal investment research.

If you are a developer, financial analyst, or business that require this level of access to Indonesian financial market data, you should consider upgrading to the Standard or Insider plans. These plans come with higher usage limits, email support, and full access to the Sectors Financial API. Paying for a subscription also helps us maintain and improve the quality of our data, and support our effort in providing accessible and affordable financial market data to the Indonesia investment landscape.
