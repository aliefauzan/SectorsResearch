## Design Your Monitoring Workflow — three steps

01 **Select Your Entities**
"Choose the main entities you want to track, including specific stocks, your personal
watchlist, or broader groups like sectors, sub-sectors, industries, and sub-industries."

02 **Define Trigger Conditions**
"Set the rules for your workflow. Trigger actions based on specific event tags, significant
price movements, or whenever your entities are mentioned in the news."

03 **Choose Your Action**
"Deliver notifications via WhatsApp, Email, Slack, Telegram, or Google Sheets—or execute a
Sectors Financial API call to fetch real-time data. Each successful execution consumes
1 Sectors credit."
