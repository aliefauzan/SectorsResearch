[Indonesia Stock Exchange Indices Performance](https://sectors.app/indonesia/indeks)

- The current value of IDX Total is IDR 11,521.58T, which represents a very slight -0.06% deviation from the previous high of IDR 11,528.08T and still points to sentiment starting to turn positive.
- Overall, the IDX Total indicates a very bullish trend for IDX stocks over the last 30 days, growing its total market capitalization from IDR 10,891.53T on August 3 to IDR 11,521.58T as of September 2, amounting to a +5.78% return.
- The positive trend in the IDX Total can be attributed to the strong performers in the [Oil, Gas & Coal](https://sectors.app/indonesia/oil-gas-coal) subsector such as [Bayan Resources](https://sectors.app/idx/byan)(+IDR67.5T), [Dian Swastatika Sentosa](https://sectors.app/idx/dssa)(+IDR54.9T),  and [Petrindo Jaya Kreasi](https://sectors.app/idx/cuan)(+IDR26.98T).

- Despite the overall growth in the IDX Total, there have been notable underperformers during this period which include [Ekamas Mora Republik](https://sectors.app/idx/mora)(-IDR-38.22T), [Multipolar Technology](https://sectors.app/idx/mlpt)(-IDR-29.3T),  and [Chandra Asri Pacific](https://sectors.app/idx/tpia)(-IDR-13.84T).
