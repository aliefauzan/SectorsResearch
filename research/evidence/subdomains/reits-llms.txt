# Sectors · REITs

> A neutral analyst terminal for Singapore-listed REITs (S-REITs) by Supertype. Every trust's FY filing fundamentals — gearing, occupancy, distribution per unit (DPU), WALE, portfolio value — alongside the live market layer (price, yield, market cap), plus where each REIT owns property, country by country. Figures are as reported in annual filings; nothing is a grade or a recommendation.

Key facts:
- Covers 37 listed Singapore REITs across 7 sub-sectors.
- Portfolios span 3420 properties in 30 countries.
- Filing data refreshes roughly yearly; the market layer is current.

## Market hubs

- [Market overview](https://reits.sectors.app/): the S-REIT market at a glance — medians, sub-sectors, geography.
- [Screener](https://reits.sectors.app/screener): every listed S-REIT in one sortable table (yield, gearing, occupancy, WALE, P/B).
- [Sub-sectors](https://reits.sectors.app/sub-sectors): the market split by asset class, defensive to cyclical.
- [Countries](https://reits.sectors.app/countries): where the rent is earned, every country ranked by exposure.
