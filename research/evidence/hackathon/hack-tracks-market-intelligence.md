# Market Intelligence

Products that turn Sectors data into insight for financial market decisions.

The qualifying test

## What must be true

The project must produce derived insight: analysis generated from the data rather than the data itself.

An AI/LLM component is optional for this track.

## What qualifies

- Signals or scores
- Rankings
- Screeners with custom logic
- Anomaly detection
- Comparative analysis
- Synthesized research outputs

## What does not qualify

A product that only displays raw Sectors data in a different visual form, however well presented, does not qualify for this track.

## Example directions

These are illustrations, not a fixed idea list. Your project can take another form if it meets the requirements above.

1. 01A custom screener that ranks companies using a team's own financial logic
2. 02An anomaly detector that surfaces unusual market or company behavior
3. 03A comparative research product that turns multiple Sectors data points into a decision-support view

Applies to every track

## The boundary rules

A project's track is determined by what the product fundamentally does, not what it looks like. An agent with a dashboard interface still belongs in AI Agents & Assistants.

An autonomous pipeline that also produces scores may fit Automation & Workflows or Market Intelligence. The team chooses the track that best represents the project's core.

If a project does not meet its declared track's requirement, judges may move it to the track that fits rather than disqualify it. Track-based disqualification applies only if the project fits no track. Teams should ask in the Slack [`#discussion`](https://join.slack.com/t/sectorshackathon/shared_invite/zt-47a8tdhhz-FgREdKQ46lUETWErcIwNcQ) channel during the build period if they are unsure.

Every track must use Sectors MCP or the Sectors REST API as a core data source. Automated trade execution is prohibited.
